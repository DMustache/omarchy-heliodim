In your bar, real size

The icon sits in the right group like the other status glyphs. It draws in the bar text color, so it follows every theme. The soft strip under it only marks it on this page.

top: 1× (12 px icon, the theme default)bottom: 2× (HiDPI)

Sun states

The icon shows the sun's elevation, not the brightness: from stars at night, through a sliver at twilight and the half disc at sunrise, up to the rayed disc near 90°. Mornings put the sun on the left, evenings on the right. Each state is shown at 4× and at real size.

Example place: Moscow, 55.75° N 37.62° E, local time UTC+3. Scrubbing drives the bar strips and the panel too.

Hero uses the logo mark at the size other Omarchy panels give their icon, with the sun's state as the meta line.

Sky plots today's elevation from midnight to midnight. The part above the horizon is the day, the dot is now, and the ticks mark sunrise, noon and sunset. It agrees with the Elsewhen daylight strip because both use the same formula.

Brightness shows the value on screen. The thin accent mark on the meter is where the sun curve alone would put it; the gap between them is your hand offset.

Buttons: pause hands brightness back to you until the next sunset or sunrise; forget offset returns to the plain curve. Both are planned.

Offset and its note are example values.

Logo

A sundial on the same 15-unit block grid as the Omarchy wordmark: the arch is the dial face and the sky, the triangle is the gnomon, and its stepped shadow points at an hour. Solid ground line underneath, like the wordmark's flat baselines.

Get it

One static Zig binary, about 230 KB, under 1 MB of memory. It sleeps until the sun moves the target brightness by one step, and keeps your hand adjustments as an offset from the curve. It writes through brightnessctl, so it needs no root. The bar icon and panel above are in progress.

git clone https://github.com/DMustache/omarchy-heliodim
cd omarchy-heliodim
zig build -Drelease --prefix ~/.local
install -Dm644 systemd/heliodim.service ~/.config/systemd/user/heliodim.service
systemctl --user enable --now heliodim
heliodim status
